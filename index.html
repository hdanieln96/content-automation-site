Content Automation

A private tool, not a public service.

Content Automation is a personal tool its owner uses to manage their own YouTube channels. It signs in with Google to read those channels' statistics (views, watch time and audience retention), which helps the owner decide what videos to make next.

It is used only by the channels' owner. Nobody else can sign up, and it does not access anyone else's account.
